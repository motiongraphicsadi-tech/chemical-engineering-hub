# What is a Tank?

A tank is a container used for storing or holding industrial fluids and materials safely.

Tanks may store:

- liquids
- gases
- slurries
- chemicals

for short-term or long-term use.

---

# Why are Tanks Important?

Industrial plants require storage systems for:

- raw materials
- intermediate products
- final products
- utilities
- waste materials

Tanks help maintain:

- operational continuity
- process flexibility
- inventory control
- emergency storage

---

# Basic Working Principle

Tanks store materials under controlled conditions.

The stored material may enter or leave the tank through:

- pumps
- pipelines
- valves

Tank systems often include:

- level monitoring
- pressure control
- venting systems
- safety systems

# Main Types of Tanks

Industrial tanks are classified based on:

- shape
- pressure condition
- storage purpose

## Common Tank Types

1. Atmospheric Tanks
2. Pressurized Tanks
3. Fixed Roof Tanks
4. Floating Roof Tanks
5. Spherical Tanks

---

# Atmospheric Tanks

Operate near atmospheric pressure.

## Applications

- water storage
- chemical storage
- fuel storage

---

# Pressurized Tanks

Designed to store fluids under pressure.

## Applications

- LPG storage
- compressed gas storage
- ammonia storage

---

# Fixed Roof Tanks

Contain permanently attached roofs.

## Advantages

- simple construction
- lower cost

## Applications

- low-volatility liquids

---

# Floating Roof Tanks

Roof floats directly on liquid surface.

## Advantages

- reduced vapor loss
- lower fire risk

## Applications

- petroleum products
- volatile liquids

<IndustrialInsight>
Floating roof tanks are widely used in refineries to reduce evaporation losses of volatile hydrocarbons.
</IndustrialInsight>

---

# Spherical Tanks

Spherical tanks are used for high-pressure gas storage.

## Advantages

- strong pressure resistance
- uniform stress distribution

## Applications

- LPG storage
- gas storage systems

---

# Venting Systems

Venting systems prevent dangerous pressure buildup.

## Common Vent Systems

- pressure relief valves
- breather valves
- flame arrestors

---

# Common Operational Problems

## Corrosion

Damages tank walls and bottom plates.

---

## Leakage

May occur due to corrosion or seal failure.

---

## Overfilling

Dangerous condition causing spills or overflow.

---

## Vapor Loss

Evaporation of volatile liquids.

<WarningBox>
Tank overfilling can lead to chemical spills, fire hazards, environmental damage, and industrial accidents.
</WarningBox>

# Summary

Tanks are essential industrial equipment used for safe storage of liquids, gases, chemicals, and process materials.

They help industries maintain:

- operational continuity
- inventory management
- process stability
- safe storage systems

Atmospheric, pressurized, floating roof, and spherical tanks are among the most common industrial tank types.

Understanding tanks is important for chemical engineers involved in storage systems, process industries, safety management, and plant operations.
