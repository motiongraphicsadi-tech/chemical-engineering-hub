## Common Operational Problems

- Pressure drop increase
- Low flow rate
- Temperature fluctuation
- Pump cavitation
- Heat exchanger fouling

---

## Troubleshooting Approach

### Step 1 — Identify Symptoms

Observe:
- Alarms
- Trends
- Process deviation

---

### Step 2 — Find Root Cause

Possible causes:
- Valve issue
- Fouling
- Instrument failure
- Utility fluctuation

---

### Step 3 — Correct Safely

Actions must avoid:
- Process instability
- Safety hazards
- Production loss
