# Introduction

Hazard Analysis is the systematic process of identifying, evaluating, and controlling potential hazards that may cause accidents, injuries, environmental damage, equipment failure, or business losses.

In chemical and process industries, hazardous materials, high pressures, high temperatures, flammable substances, toxic chemicals, and complex operations create risks that must be carefully managed.

Hazard analysis provides engineers and safety professionals with structured techniques to identify potential dangers before incidents occur and implement safeguards that keep people, equipment, and the environment safe.

Modern process safety management relies heavily on hazard analysis to achieve safe, reliable, and efficient plant operation.

---

# Why Hazard Analysis Matters

Process industries handle enormous quantities of hazardous materials every day.

Without proper hazard identification and risk assessment, incidents can lead to:

- Loss of life
- Serious injuries
- Environmental pollution
- Equipment damage
- Production losses
- Regulatory violations
- Financial losses
- Damage to company reputation

Hazard analysis helps organizations move from reactive safety management to proactive risk prevention.

# Key Safety Philosophy

A major objective of hazard analysis is to answer three fundamental questions:

### What Can Go Wrong?

Hazard identification.

### How Likely Is It?

Risk evaluation.

### What Can Be Done About It?

Risk reduction and safeguard implementation.

Every hazard analysis technique discussed in this section ultimately supports these three objectives.
