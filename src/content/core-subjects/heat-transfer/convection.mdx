## What is Convection?

Convection is the mode of heat transfer that occurs between a surface and a moving fluid.

Unlike conduction:

- conduction occurs through molecular interaction
- convection requires fluid motion

Examples include:
- air flowing over a hot pipe
- water cooling a reactor wall
- steam heating process fluids

<IndustrialInsight>
Convection dominates heat transfer in most industrial heat exchangers, cooling towers, condensers, reboilers, and fired heaters.
</IndustrialInsight>

---

## Types of Convection

### Natural Convection

Fluid motion occurs naturally due to density differences caused by temperature variation.

Examples:
- hot air rising
- room cooling
- chimney draft

### Forced Convection

Fluid motion is generated externally using:
- pumps
- blowers
- fans
- compressors

Examples:
- shell and tube heat exchangers
- cooling water circulation
- forced air cooling systems

<WarningBox>
Poor convection performance can significantly reduce heat exchanger efficiency and increase energy consumption.
</WarningBox>

## Newton’s Law of Cooling

Convection heat transfer is governed by Newton’s Law of Cooling.

<EquationBlock
  title="Newton's Law of Cooling"
  equation="Q = hA(T_s - T_f)"
  description="Heat transfer rate depends on convection coefficient, area, and temperature difference."
/>


## Understanding the Equation


- **Q** → Heat transfer rate  
- **h** → Convective heat transfer coefficient  
- **A** → Surface area  
- **T_s** → Surface temperature  
- **T_f** → Fluid temperature

## Factors Affecting Convection

### Fluid Velocity

Higher velocity:
- increases turbulence
- reduces boundary layer thickness
- improves heat transfer

### Fluid Properties

Important properties include:
- viscosity
- density
- thermal conductivity
- specific heat

### Surface Geometry

Surface shape strongly affects:
- flow behavior
- turbulence
- convection coefficient

## Industrial Applications

Convection is heavily used in:

- shell and tube heat exchangers
- air coolers
- condensers
- evaporators
- reboilers
- reactor cooling jackets

<IndustrialInsight>
In industrial plants, improving convection performance directly reduces utility costs and improves process efficiency.
</IndustrialInsight>

---

## Boundary Layer Concept

Near the solid surface, fluid velocity becomes very small.

This region is called the:

- thermal boundary layer
- velocity boundary layer

Heat transfer resistance is strongly affected by boundary layer thickness.
