# What is a Filter?

A filter is equipment used to separate solid particles from fluids using a filtering medium.

The filter allows:

- clean fluid to pass through
- solid particles to remain trapped

Filtration may involve:

- liquid-solid separation
- gas-solid separation

---

# Why are Filters Important?

Industrial fluids often contain impurities such as:

- dust
- rust
- sludge
- catalyst particles
- suspended solids

Filters help:

- improve product purity
- protect equipment
- maintain process quality
- reduce maintenance problems

---

# Basic Working Principle

Filtration works by passing fluid through a filtering medium.

## Basic Steps

1. contaminated fluid enters filter
2. filter medium traps particles
3. clean fluid exits filter
4. collected solids remain inside filter

# Filtration Mechanisms

## Surface Filtration

## Depth Filtration

Industrial filters are classified based on application and design.

## Common Filter Types

1. Cartridge Filters

6. Membrane Filters

# Cartridge Filters

Use replaceable filter cartridges.

## Advantages

- compact design
- easy replacement
- fine filtration capability

## Applications

- water treatment
- chemical processing
- pharmaceutical industries

---

# Bag Filters

Use filter bags to collect particles.

## Advantages

- simple operation
- high dirt holding capacity

## Applications

- liquid filtration
- slurry systems

---

# Sand Filters

Use sand layers for filtration.

## Applications

- water treatment
- cooling water systems

---

# Vacuum Filters

Use vacuum pressure to separate solids from liquids.

## Applications

- mining industries
- slurry filtration
- chemical processing

---

# Air Filters

Remove dust and particles from air systems.

## Applications

- HVAC systems
- compressor intake systems
- clean rooms

# Filtration Performance Parameters

## Filtration Efficiency

Ability to remove particles.

---

# Filter Clogging

Filters gradually accumulate solids and become clogged.

## Effects

- reduced flow rate
- increased pressure drop
- poor filtration performance

Regular cleaning or replacement is required.

<WarningBox>
Excessive filter clogging can restrict process flow and damage downstream equipment.
</WarningBox>
