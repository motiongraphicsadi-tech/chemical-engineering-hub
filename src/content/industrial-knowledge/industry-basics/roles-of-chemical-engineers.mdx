# Who is a Chemical Engineer?

A chemical engineer is an engineer who designs, operates, optimizes, and manages industrial processes involving chemical and physical transformations.

Chemical engineers apply engineering principles to:

- convert raw materials into useful products
- improve industrial efficiency
- ensure process safety
- optimize production systems

---

1. Process Engineer
2. Production Engineer
3. Plant Operations Engineer

# Process Engineer

Process engineers optimize industrial processes for:

- higher efficiency
- lower cost
- stable operation
- better safety

## Major Responsibilities

- process monitoring
- troubleshooting
- energy optimization
- heat and mass balance
- process calculations
- plant optimization

## Common Work Areas

- reactors
- distillation columns
- heat exchangers
- pipelines
- utilities systems

---

# Production Engineer

Production engineers ensure smooth plant operation and target achievement.

## Major Responsibilities

- production planning
- shift coordination
- operational supervision
- production monitoring
- troubleshooting

## Important Focus Areas

- production efficiency
- process stability
- downtime reduction

# Plant Operations Engineer

Operations engineers directly support plant operation activities.

## Responsibilities

- startup and shutdown operation
- operator coordination
- process parameter monitoring
- field inspection
- emergency response

Plant operations engineers often work closely with field operators and control room operators during real-time plant operation.

# Safety Engineer

Safety engineers maintain industrial safety standards.

## Responsibilities

- hazard analysis
- risk assessment
- safety audits
- permit systems
- emergency planning
- accident investigation

## Common Safety Studies

- HAZOP
- risk analysis
- fire and explosion studies

</WarningBox>
