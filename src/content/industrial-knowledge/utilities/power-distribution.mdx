# Introduction

Power distribution systems are essential industrial utility systems used to distribute electrical power safely throughout industrial plants.

Electrical power is required for operating:

- pumps
- compressors
- motors
- reactors
- control systems
- lighting systems

Power distribution systems are critical in:

- refineries
- fertilizer plants
- power plants
- pharmaceutical industries
- chemical plants

Reliable power systems help maintain:

- continuous operation
- process safety
- equipment reliability
- plant productivity

---

<IndustrialInsight>
Even a short power interruption can cause major production loss and safety risks in large process industries.
</IndustrialInsight>

# What is a Power Distribution System?

A power distribution system is an industrial electrical network used to distribute electricity from the power source to different plant equipment and systems.

The system ensures:

- safe power delivery
- voltage control
- equipment protection
- continuous electrical supply

---

# Why are Power Distribution Systems Important?

Industrial plants require electricity for almost every operation.

Power distribution systems help:

- operate industrial equipment
- support automation systems
- maintain plant operation
- improve process reliability

Without reliable electrical power, industrial plants cannot function properly.

# Voltage Levels in Industries

Industrial plants use different voltage levels depending on equipment requirements.

## Common Voltage Categories

- high voltage (HV)
- medium voltage (MV)
- low voltage (LV)

---

# Transformers

Transformers increase or decrease voltage levels.

## Functions

- step-up voltage
- step-down voltage
- improve power transmission efficiency

<IndustrialInsight>
Transformers are critical equipment because industrial equipment operates at different voltage levels.
</IndustrialInsight>

# Switchgear

Switchgear controls and isolates electrical equipment.

## Functions

- power switching
- fault isolation
- equipment protection

---

# Circuit Breakers

Circuit breakers automatically disconnect faulty electrical circuits.

## Protection Against

- overloads
- short circuits
- electrical faults

---

# Distribution Panels

Distribution panels divide electrical power into smaller circuits for:
