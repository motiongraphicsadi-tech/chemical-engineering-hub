# What is a Chilled Water System?

A chilled water system is a cooling utility system that circulates cold water to remove heat from industrial equipment or air-conditioning systems.

The chilled water absorbs heat and returns to the chiller for recooling.

---

# Why are Chilled Water Systems Important?

Some industrial processes require lower temperatures than standard cooling water can provide.

Chilled water systems help maintain:

- controlled process temperature
- product quality
- equipment protection
- environmental comfort

---

# Basic Working Principle

## Step 1

Chiller cools water to low temperature.

---

## Step 2

Chilled water circulates through equipment or air handling units.

---

## Step 3

Water absorbs heat from the process or surrounding air.

---

## Step 4

Warm water returns to the chiller.

---

## Step 5

Chiller removes absorbed heat and cools water again.

# Types of Chillers

## Air-Cooled Chiller

Uses air to remove condenser heat.

### Advantages

- simpler installation
- no cooling tower required

---

## Water-Cooled Chiller

Uses cooling water and cooling towers.

### Advantages

- higher efficiency
- better performance for large systems

<IndustrialInsight>
Water-cooled chillers are commonly used in large industrial plants because of their higher cooling efficiency.
</IndustrialInsight>

# Common Operational Problems

## Fouling

Reduces heat transfer efficiency.

---

## Refrigerant Leakage

Causes poor cooling performance.

---

## Pump Failure

## Low Cooling Capacity

---

## Scaling

Reduces heat exchanger efficiency.
