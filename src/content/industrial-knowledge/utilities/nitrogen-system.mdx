# Introduction

Nitrogen systems are important industrial utility systems used to supply nitrogen gas for:

- inerting
- blanketing
- purging
- pressure transfer
- safety applications

Nitrogen is widely used because it is:

- non-flammable
- chemically inert
- dry
- relatively safe for industrial use

Nitrogen systems are essential in:

- refineries
- petrochemical plants
- pharmaceutical industries
- fertilizer plants
- storage facilities

# What is a Nitrogen System?

A nitrogen system is an industrial utility system used to generate, store, and distribute nitrogen gas throughout the plant.

Nitrogen is mainly used to create:

# Inert Atmospheres

inside industrial equipment and pipelines.

---

# Why are Nitrogen Systems Important?

Many industrial materials are:

- flammable
- reactive
- oxygen-sensitive

Nitrogen systems help:

- prevent fires
- reduce explosion risk
- protect product quality
- improve process safety

## Liquid Nitrogen Storage

## Nitrogen Generators

Generate nitrogen onsite.

### Common Technologies

- PSA systems
- membrane systems
- cryogenic separation

# PSA Nitrogen System

PSA means:

# Pressure Swing Adsorption

This system separates nitrogen from air using adsorption materials.

## Advantages

- onsite nitrogen generation
- lower operating cost
- continuous supply

<IndustrialInsight>
PSA nitrogen generators are widely used in modern industries for reliable onsite nitrogen production.

# Nitrogen Blanketing

Nitrogen blanketing uses nitrogen gas to cover liquid surfaces inside tanks.

## Purpose

- reduce oxygen contact
- prevent oxidation
- minimize fire risk

## Applications

- fuel tanks
- solvent storage
- chemical tanks

---

# Nitrogen Purging

Nitrogen purging removes unwanted gases from pipelines or equipment.

## Applications

- startup operations
- shutdown operations
- maintenance preparation

---

# Nitrogen Inerting

Nitrogen inerting creates oxygen-free environments.

## Benefits

- fire prevention
- explosion prevention
- process safety improvement

---

# Pressure Transfer Applications

Nitrogen pressure may be used for:

- chemical transfer
- tank unloading
- pipeline clearing

---

# Nitrogen Quality

Industrial nitrogen quality depends on:

- purity
- moisture level
- oxygen content

Different applications require different nitrogen purities.

# Common Operational Problems

## Leakage

Causes nitrogen loss and pressure reduction.

---

## Pressure Fluctuation

Affects system performance.

---

## Moisture Contamination

Reduces nitrogen quality.

---

## Low Purity

May create safety risks.

<WarningBox>
Nitrogen gas can displace oxygen in confined spaces and create serious asphyxiation hazards.
</WarningBox>

---

# Nitrogen Safety Considerations

Nitrogen is non-toxic but dangerous in confined spaces.

## Common Risks

- oxygen deficiency
- asphyxiation
- pressure hazards
- cold burns from liquid nitrogen

## Important Safety Practices

- oxygen monitoring
- proper ventilation
- PPE usage
- confined space procedures

---

# Liquid Nitrogen Hazards

Liquid nitrogen operates at extremely low temperatures.

## Risks

- frostbite
- material brittleness
- rapid gas expansion

---
