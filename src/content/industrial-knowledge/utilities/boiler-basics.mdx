# What is a Boiler?

A boiler is a closed vessel used to heat water and generate steam using heat energy.

The generated steam is distributed to industrial systems for heating and utility applications.

---

# Why are Boilers Important?

Industries require steam for:

- process heating
- energy transfer
- turbine operation
- chemical processing
- utility systems

Boilers help maintain:

- continuous steam supply
- process efficiency
- operational stability

---

# Basic Working Principle

## Step 1

Fuel burns inside the boiler furnace.

---

## Step 2

Heat transfers to water inside tubes or vessels.

---

## Step 3

Water converts into steam.

---

## Step 4

Steam collects and distributes to process systems.

# Main Components of a Boiler

Industrial boilers contain several important components.

## Furnace

Area where fuel combustion occurs.

---

## Burner

Supplies and burns fuel.

---

## Boiler Tubes

Transfer heat between hot gases and water.

---

## Steam Drum

Separates steam from water.

---

## Economizer

Preheats feedwater using waste heat.

---

## Superheater

Raises steam temperature above saturation temperature.

---

## Chimney or Stack

Removes flue gases safely.

# Steam Generation

Water absorbs heat energy and changes phase into steam.

# Types of Steam

## Saturated Steam

Steam in equilibrium with water.

Used mainly for process heating.

---

## Superheated Steam

Steam heated above saturation temperature.

Used mainly for turbines and power generation.

# Main Types of Boilers

Industrial boilers are mainly classified into:

1. Fire Tube Boilers
2. Water Tube Boilers

---

# Fire Tube Boiler

In fire tube boilers:

- hot gases flow inside tubes
- water surrounds the tubes

## Advantages

- simple construction
- low cost
- easy operation

## Applications

- low-pressure systems
- small industries

---

# Water Tube Boiler

In water tube boilers:

- water flows inside tubes
- hot gases surround tubes

## Advantages

- high-pressure operation
- higher efficiency
- high steam generation rate

## Applications

- power plants
- large process industries

<IndustrialInsight>
Water tube boilers are widely used in large industries because they can safely generate high-pressure steam.
</IndustrialInsight>

# Boiler Feedwater System

Boilers require treated feedwater.

Feedwater treatment removes:

- oxygen
- impurities

Proper feedwater quality prevents:

- scaling
- corrosion
- tube damage

# Common Operational Problems

## Scaling

Mineral deposits reduce heat transfer.

---

## Corrosion

Damages boiler tubes and internals.

---

## Tube Failure

Occurs due to overheating or corrosion.

---

## Flame Failure

Combustion stops unexpectedly.

---

## Low Water Level

Extremely dangerous boiler condition.

<WarningBox>
Low water level can cause severe overheating and possible boiler explosion.
</WarningBox>

---

# Boiler Blowdown

Boiler blowdown removes dissolved solids and impurities from boiler water.

## Benefits

- improves water quality
- prevents scaling
- protects boiler components
