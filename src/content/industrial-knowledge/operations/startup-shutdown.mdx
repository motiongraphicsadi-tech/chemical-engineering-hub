## Why Startup Is Critical

Improper startup can cause:
- Thermal shock
- Pressure surge
- Equipment damage
- Safety incidents

---

## Typical Startup Sequence

1. Utility availability
2. Instrument checks
3. Pump startup
4. Flow establishment
5. Heating sequence
6. Stabilization

---

## Shutdown Types

### Planned Shutdown
Performed for maintenance.

### Emergency Shutdown
Triggered automatically during unsafe conditions.

---

<WarningBox title="Operational Risk">

Rapid heating and cooling can severely damage equipment.

</WarningBox>

---

## Key Takeaway

Startup and shutdown procedures are among the highest-risk plant operations.
