## Scenario

A reactor temperature increased rapidly due to cooling failure.

The emergency shutdown system activated automatically.

---

## Events

- Cooling water stopped
- Reactor temperature increased
- High temperature alarm triggered
- Emergency shutdown activated

---

## Root Cause

Cooling water pump failure.

---

## Engineering Lessons

- Utility reliability is critical
- Backup systems are necessary
- Alarm systems save equipment and lives

---

<WarningBox title="High Risk Situation">

Runaway reactions can become catastrophic without rapid shutdown systems.

</WarningBox>

---

## Key Takeaway

Emergency shutdown systems are essential safety layers in chemical plants.
