- refineries
- pharmaceutical industries

- separation efficiency

# What is a Vacuum System?

A vacuum system is an industrial utility system used to create and maintain low-pressure conditions below atmospheric pressure.

Vacuum systems remove gases or vapors from enclosed equipment or pipelines.

---

# Why are Vacuum Systems Important?

Many industrial processes require:

- low boiling temperatures
- oxygen-free environments
- moisture removal
- gas removal

Vacuum systems help:

- improve product quality
- reduce thermal damage
- increase separation efficiency
- support sensitive operations

## Step 2

## Step 4

# Vacuum Pumps

Vacuum pumps are the heart of vacuum systems.

They remove gases from the process equipment to create low pressure.

---

# Common Types of Vacuum Pumps

## Liquid Ring Vacuum Pump

Uses liquid sealing inside rotating impeller.

### Advantages

- reliable operation
- handles wet gases

### Applications

- chemical industries
- vacuum distillation

---

## Rotary Vane Vacuum Pump

Uses rotating vanes for gas compression.

### Applications

- laboratory systems
- small industrial systems

---

## Steam Jet Ejector

Uses high-pressure steam to create vacuum.

### Applications

- refineries
- large vacuum systems

<IndustrialInsight>
Steam jet ejectors are widely used in refineries for vacuum distillation operations.
</IndustrialInsight>

# Condensers in Vacuum Systems

- protect vacuum pumps
- reduce operating cost

# Important Vacuum Parameters

## Vacuum Level

Indicates pressure below atmospheric pressure.

---

## Pump Capacity

Amount of gas removed per unit time.

---

## Leakage Rate

Amount of unwanted air entering the system.

---

# Common Operational Problems

## Air Leakage

Reduces vacuum efficiency.

---

## Pump Failure

Interrupts vacuum generation.

---

## Seal Failure

Causes pressure instability.

---

## Condenser Fouling

Reduces vapor condensation efficiency.

---

## Excessive Vapor Load

Overloads vacuum pumps.

<WarningBox>
Air leakage is one of the most common causes of poor vacuum system performance in industries.
</WarningBox>
