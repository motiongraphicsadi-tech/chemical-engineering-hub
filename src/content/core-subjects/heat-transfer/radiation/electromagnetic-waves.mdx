# Electromagnetic Waves

<TopicRoadmap />

## What Carries Thermal Radiation?

If radiation can travel through a vacuum, what actually carries the energy?

The answer is:

> Electromagnetic Waves

Thermal radiation is simply thermal energy transported through electromagnetic waves.

These waves can travel through:

- Solids
- Liquids
- Gases
- Vacuum

This is why energy from the Sun can reach the Earth through empty space.

---

# What Is an Electromagnetic Wave?

An electromagnetic wave consists of:

- An oscillating electric field
- An oscillating magnetic field

Both fields continuously regenerate each other and travel through space carrying energy.

---

Magnetic Field (B)

     ⊙ ⊗ ⊙ ⊗

(Perpendicular to Electric Field)

Key idea:

- Electric field and magnetic field are perpendicular to each other.
- Both are perpendicular to the direction of wave travel.

---

# Wave Propagation

Once generated, electromagnetic waves propagate through space carrying energy from one location to another.

Examples:

- Sun → Earth
- Furnace → Process Tube
- Hot Reactor → Surroundings
- Infrared Heater → Human Body

No material medium is required.

---

# Speed of Electromagnetic Waves

Electromagnetic waves travel at the speed of light.

$$
c = 3 \times 10^8 \; m/s
$$

Where:

- \(c\) = Speed of light

This is approximately:

- 300,000 km/s
- 186,000 miles/s

As a result, thermal radiation reaches nearby surfaces almost instantaneously.

---

# Thermal Radiation as Electromagnetic Waves

When a hot object emits radiation:

```text
Hot Surface
     ↓
Electromagnetic Waves
     ↓
Cold Surface
```

Energy leaves the hot surface, travels through space, and is absorbed by the colder surface.

This process is the foundation of radiation heat transfer.

---

# Engineering Examples

## The Sun

Thermal energy travels millions of kilometers through space before reaching Earth.

---

## Furnace Tubes

Radiation from flames is carried by electromagnetic waves and absorbed by process tubes.

---

## Infrared Heaters

Infrared waves directly transfer energy to people and objects.

Infrared cameras detect electromagnetic waves emitted by hot surfaces.

# Key Takeaways

- Thermal radiation is carried by electromagnetic waves.
- Electromagnetic waves consist of electric and magnetic fields.
- The electric and magnetic fields are perpendicular to each other.
- Electromagnetic waves do not require a material medium.
- They travel at the speed of light.
- Radiation heat transfer occurs because thermal energy is transported by electromagnetic waves.

---

# Summary

Thermal radiation is transported through electromagnetic waves composed of oscillating electric and magnetic fields. These waves can travel through solids, liquids, gases, and even vacuum without requiring a material medium. Since electromagnetic waves move at the speed of light, radiation heat transfer occurs extremely rapidly and forms the basis of energy transfer from the Sun, furnaces, infrared heaters, and other high-temperature systems.
