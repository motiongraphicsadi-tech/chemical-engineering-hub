# Introduction

Cooling towers are important industrial utility equipment used to remove unwanted heat from process systems.

They cool hot water by transferring heat to the atmosphere.

Cooling towers are essential in:

- refineries
- petrochemical plants
- power plants
- fertilizer industries
- HVAC systems
- chemical plants

Without cooling towers, industrial equipment may overheat, causing:

- process instability
- equipment damage
- safety risks

# What is a Cooling Tower?

A cooling tower is industrial equipment used to cool hot water by rejecting heat into the atmosphere.

Cooling towers are part of the:

# Cooling Water System

Hot water from industrial equipment is sent to the cooling tower and cooled before recirculation.

---

# Why are Cooling Towers Important?

Industrial equipment generates large amounts of heat.

Cooling towers help maintain:

- safe operating temperatures
- process stability
- equipment protection
- continuous operation

Cooling systems are critical for:

- condensers
- heat exchangers
- compressors
- reactors
- turbines

---

# Basic Working Principle

Cooling towers mainly use:

# Evaporative Cooling

## Basic Steps

1. hot water enters the cooling tower
2. water spreads over fill material
3. air flows through the tower
4. small portion of water evaporates
5. remaining water cools down

The cooled water is then recirculated back to the process.

# Heat Removal Mechanism

Cooling towers remove heat through:

## Evaporation

Small amount of water evaporates and carries away heat.

---

## Sensible Cooling

Heat transfers directly from water to air.

# Main Types of Cooling Towers

Industrial cooling towers are mainly classified into:

1. Natural Draft Cooling Towers
2. Mechanical Draft Cooling Towers

---

# Natural Draft Cooling Tower

Uses natural air circulation due to density differences.

## Characteristics

- very large structure
- no fan required
- used in power plants

---

# Mechanical Draft Cooling Tower

Uses fans to force air movement.

## Main Types

- induced draft tower
- forced draft tower

## Advantages

- compact design
- better cooling control
- widely used in industries

<IndustrialInsight>
Mechanical draft cooling towers are the most commonly used cooling towers in modern process industries.
</IndustrialInsight>

# Major Cooling Tower Components

## Fan

Moves air through the tower.

---

## Fill Material

Increases contact area between air and water.

---

## Drift Eliminator

Reduces water droplet loss.

---

## Spray Nozzles

Distribute hot water evenly.

---

## Basin

---

## Louvers

# Cooling Tower Performance Parameters

## Cooling Range

Difference between hot water temperature and cooled water temperature.

---

## Approach

Difference between cooled water temperature and wet bulb temperature.

---

## Efficiency

Indicates cooling tower effectiveness.

# Water Losses in Cooling Towers

Cooling towers experience:

## Evaporation Loss

Water lost during evaporation.

---

## Drift Loss

Water droplets carried away with air.

---

## Blowdown Loss

Water removed to control impurities.

# Cooling Tower Water Treatment

Water treatment prevents:

- scaling
- corrosion
- biological growth

## Common Treatments

- chemical dosing
- biocides
- pH control
- filtration

<WarningBox>
Poor cooling water treatment may cause severe scaling, corrosion, and biological contamination in cooling systems.
</WarningBox>
