#  Steady and Unsteady State Heat Transfer

# Introduction

Heat transfer systems may operate under steady or unsteady conditions depending on whether temperature changes with time.

Understanding these concepts is important for:
- heat exchanger analysis
- reactor heating
- cooling systems
- startup and shutdown operations
- transient heat transfer calculations

<IndustrialInsight>
  Most industrial plants aim for steady-state operation because it provides stable production and easier process control.
</IndustrialInsight>

---

## 3. Define steady state heat transfer?

<InterviewQuestion
  question="What is steady state heat transfer?"
  answer="Steady-state heat transfer is a condition where temperature does not change with time at any location in the system."
/>

## Characteristics of Steady State

- temperature remains constant with time
- heat transfer rate remains constant
- no energy accumulation occurs

## Industrial Examples

- continuous heat exchanger operation
- boiler operating at constant load
- stable distillation column operation

<EquationBlock
  title="Steady-State Condition"
  equation="dT/dt = 0"
  description="In steady-state heat transfer, temperature does not change with time."
/>

---

## 4. Define unsteady state heat transfer?

<InterviewQuestion
  question="What is unsteady state heat transfer?"
  answer="Unsteady-state heat transfer is a condition where temperature changes with time."
/>

## Characteristics of Unsteady State

- temperature varies with time
- heat transfer rate changes continuously
- energy accumulation occurs

## Industrial Examples

- plant startup
- plant shutdown
- heating of storage tanks
- cooling of hot metal objects

<EquationBlock
  title="Unsteady-State Condition"
  equation="dT/dt ≠ 0"
  description="In unsteady-state heat transfer, temperature changes with time."
/>

<WarningBox>
  Unsteady-state heat transfer is also called transient heat transfer.
</WarningBox>

---

## 5. Example from industry?

## Steady-State Examples

- shell and tube heat exchanger
- refinery distillation column
- continuous evaporator system
- utility steam line

## Unsteady-State Examples

- reactor startup heating
- cooling tower startup
- furnace shutdown cooling
- batch reactor heating

<IndustrialInsight>
  Startup and shutdown conditions in chemical plants are usually unsteady-state operations because temperatures and process conditions continuously change.
</IndustrialInsight>

## 6. What is lumped heat transfer?

<InterviewQuestion
  question="What is lumped heat transfer?"
  answer="Lumped heat transfer assumes the entire body remains at uniform temperature during heating or cooling."
/>

# Lumped System Analysis

In lumped heat transfer:
- temperature inside the body is assumed uniform
- internal temperature gradient is neglected
- temperature changes only with time

## Conditions for Lumped Analysis

Lumped analysis is valid when internal conduction resistance is very small compared to external convection resistance.

## Applications

- small metal objects
- thermocouples
- electronic components
- small spherical particles

---

## 6.1 What is Biot number?

<InterviewQuestion
  question="What is Biot number?"
  answer="Biot number is the ratio of internal conduction resistance to external convection resistance."
/>

<EquationBlock
  title="Biot Number"
  equation="Bi = hL/k"
  description="Biot number compares internal conduction resistance with external convection resistance."
/>

Where:
- h = heat transfer coefficient
- L = characteristic length
- k = thermal conductivity

## Important Condition

<EquationBlock
  title="Condition for Lumped Heat Transfer"
  equation="Bi < 0.1"
  description="If Biot number is less than 0.1, lumped heat transfer analysis is valid."
/>

## Importance of Biot Number

Biot number helps determine:
- whether lumped analysis is valid
- temperature distribution inside solids
- transient heat transfer behavior

<IndustrialInsight>
  Engineers use Biot number in transient heating and cooling calculations for industrial equipment and solid materials.
</IndustrialInsight>

# Summary

Steady and unsteady heat transfer are fundamental concepts in heat transfer analysis.

Key points:
- steady state means temperature does not change with time
- unsteady state means temperature changes with time
- lumped analysis assumes uniform temperature inside the body
- Biot number determines validity of lumped heat transfer analysis
- these concepts are widely used in industrial heat transfer systems
