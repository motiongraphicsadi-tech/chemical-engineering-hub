## Scenario

A heat exchanger network was started after maintenance shutdown.

During startup:
- Flow instability occurred
- Pressure surged
- Temperature fluctuated

---

## Root Cause

Operators increased steam flow too rapidly.

This caused:
- Thermal stress
- Unstable control response
- Pressure oscillation

---

## Engineering Lessons

- Startup must be gradual
- Utilities must stabilize first
- Control loops require tuning

---

<IndustrialInsight title="Real Industry Insight">

Most startup problems happen because systems are pushed too quickly before stabilization.

</IndustrialInsight>

---

## Key Takeaway

Controlled startup sequencing is critical for plant safety and reliability.
