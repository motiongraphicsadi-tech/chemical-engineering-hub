## What is Conduction?

Conduction is the mode of heat transfer that occurs through direct molecular interaction within a material without bulk movement of the material itself.

Heat always flows: from higher temperature to lower temperature .

until thermal equilibrium is reached.

---

## Everyday Examples

- Heating of a metal spoon inside hot tea
- Heat transfer through reactor walls
- Heat loss from pipelines
- Heat transfer through insulation

<IndustrialInsight>
In industrial systems, conduction directly affects heat exchanger efficiency, insulation performance, furnace operation, and reactor thermal stability.
</IndustrialInsight>
