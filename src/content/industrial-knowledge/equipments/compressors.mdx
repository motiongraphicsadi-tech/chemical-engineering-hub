# Introduction

Compressors are important industrial equipment used to increase the pressure of gases by reducing their volume.

They are widely used in process industries for handling gases such as:

- air
- nitrogen
- hydrogen
- natural gas
- process gases

Compressors are essential in:

- refineries
- petrochemical plants
- fertilizer industries
- power plants
- gas processing industries

# What is a Compressor?

A compressor is a mechanical device used to increase gas pressure by compressing gas into a smaller volume.

Compressors help transport gases through:

- pipelines
- reactors
- processing units
- storage systems

---

# Why are Compressors Important?

Industrial processes require compressed gases for:

- pneumatic systems
- refrigeration
- chemical reactions
- gas transportation
- combustion systems

Compressors help maintain:

- gas flow
- process pressure
- operational continuity

---

# Basic Working Principle

Compressors operate by:

1. taking gas at low pressure
2. reducing gas volume
3. increasing gas pressure

Mechanical energy from motors or turbines is converted into pressure energy.

# Main Types of Compressors

Industrial compressors are mainly classified into:

1. Positive Displacement Compressors
2. Dynamic Compressors

---

# Positive Displacement Compressors

These compressors trap a fixed amount of gas and reduce its volume.

## Common Types

- reciprocating compressors
- rotary screw compressors
- rotary vane compressors

---

# Reciprocating Compressors

Reciprocating compressors use pistons moving inside cylinders.

## Working Principle

- gas enters cylinder
- piston compresses gas
- compressed gas exits through discharge valve

## Advantages

- high pressure capability
- suitable for low flow rates

## Applications

- gas cylinders
- process gas systems
- refrigeration systems

---

# Rotary Screw Compressors

These compressors use rotating screws to compress gases.

## Advantages

- continuous operation
- low vibration
- compact design

## Applications

- compressed air systems
- industrial utilities

---

# Dynamic Compressors

Dynamic compressors increase gas velocity and convert velocity into pressure.

## Main Types

- centrifugal compressors
- axial compressors

---

# Centrifugal Compressors

Centrifugal compressors use rotating impellers.

## Working Principle

- gas enters impeller
- velocity increases
- diffuser converts velocity into pressure

## Advantages

- high flow capability
- smooth continuous flow
- low maintenance

## Applications

- refineries
- petrochemical plants
- natural gas systems

<IndustrialInsight>
Centrifugal compressors are commonly used in large continuous process industries because of their high flow handling capability.
</IndustrialInsight>

- single-stage compression
- multi-stage compression

# Compressor Performance Parameters

## Pressure Ratio

Ratio of discharge pressure to suction pressure.

---

## Flow Rate

Amount of gas compressed per unit time.

---

## Efficiency

Indicates compressor performance effectiveness.

---

## Power Consumption

Energy required for gas compression.

---

# Heat Generation During Compression

Gas temperature increases during compression.

Therefore compressors often require:

- intercoolers
- aftercoolers
- cooling systems

---

# Surge in Compressors

Surge is a dangerous instability condition in compressors.

## Causes

- low flow condition
- improper operation
- process disturbance

## Effects

- vibration
- pressure fluctuation
- equipment damage

<WarningBox>
Compressor surge can severely damage rotating equipment if not controlled properly.
</WarningBox>

## Leakage
