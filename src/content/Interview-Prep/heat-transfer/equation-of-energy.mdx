# A) Equation of Energy

# Introduction

The equation of energy is one of the most important concepts in heat transfer and chemical engineering.

It is based on the conservation of energy principle, which states that energy can neither be created nor destroyed.

Energy balance equations are used in:
- heat exchangers
- boilers
- reactors
- evaporators
- condensers
- industrial utility systems

<IndustrialInsight>
  Energy balance calculations are widely used in chemical industries for process design, equipment sizing, and troubleshooting.
</IndustrialInsight>

---

## 1. What is the general energy balance?

<InterviewQuestion
  question="What is the general energy balance?"
  answer="General energy balance states that accumulation equals input minus output plus generation minus consumption."
/>

## General Energy Balance Equation

<EquationBlock
  title="General Energy Balance"
  equation="Accumulation = Input - Output + Generation - Consumption"
  description="General conservation of energy equation used in process systems."
/>

## Importance in Industry

Energy balance is used in:
- heat exchanger calculations
- steam generation systems
- refrigeration systems
- reactor energy calculations
- evaporator systems

<IndustrialInsight>
  Engineers use energy balance equations to calculate heating duty, cooling load, steam consumption, and process efficiency.
</IndustrialInsight>

---

## 2. When do we get accumulation = 0?

<InterviewQuestion
  question="When does accumulation become zero?"
  answer="Accumulation becomes zero during steady-state operation when system properties do not change with time."
/>

## Steady-State Condition

<EquationBlock
  title="Steady-State Energy Equation"
  equation="dE/dt = 0"
  description="At steady state, there is no energy accumulation inside the system."
/>

## Meaning of Steady State

At steady state:
- no energy buildup occurs
- no energy depletion occurs
- system properties remain constant with time

## Industrial Examples

### Steady-State Examples

- continuous heat exchanger operation
- boiler operating at constant load
- stable distillation column operation

### Unsteady-State Examples

- plant startup
- plant shutdown
- batch heating process

<WarningBox>
  During startup and shutdown conditions, accumulation is usually not zero because temperature and operating conditions change with time.
</WarningBox>
