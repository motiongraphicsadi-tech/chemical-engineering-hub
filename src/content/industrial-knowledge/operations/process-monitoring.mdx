## Important Parameters

Operators monitor:
- Temperature
- Pressure
- Flow
- Level
- Composition

---

## Monitoring Systems

### DCS
Distributed Control System used for centralized monitoring.

### Field Instruments
Sensors installed directly in the plant.

---

## Why Monitoring Matters

Good monitoring helps:
- Prevent shutdowns
- Detect abnormalities early
- Improve safety
- Maintain quality

---

<IndustrialInsight title="Plant Reality">

Experienced operators can often detect problems from small trend changes before alarms occur.

</IndustrialInsight>

---

## Key Takeaway

Process monitoring is the backbone of stable plant operation.
